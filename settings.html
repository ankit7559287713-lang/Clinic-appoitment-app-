Known limitations

  • Data lives in this browser’s LocalStorage. It isn’t shared across devices or browsers.
  • LocalStorage isn’t a transactional database. Bookings re-check right before saving and tabs refresh on change, but true multi-user booking needs a server with a unique constraint per slot.
  • Roles are a demonstration login, not security.
  • All names and phone numbers are fictional. Don’t enter real health information.
