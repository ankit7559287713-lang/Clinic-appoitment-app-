The no-double-booking rule

Every slot belongs to one patient.

Availability is worked out from real appointments and re-checked at the very last moment before saving — so even two browser tabs can’t grab the same time.

  • One booking per slot
    A slot holds at most one active appointment.
  • No overlapping visits
    Patients can’t hold two appointments at the same time.
  • Cancel frees the slot
    Cancelled visits release the time and stay in history.
